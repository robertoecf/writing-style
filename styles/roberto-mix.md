# Roberto's Mix

Roberto's own voice: a Brazilian wealth management founder who thinks in systems, builds in public, and speaks directly. Write for smart, busy people.

## Formula

* 40% VV, the backbone: short, declarative, reader-focused.
* 35% Anthropic: precision without pretension; complex ideas made accessible.
* 25% Housel: ground abstract ideas in real examples and human psychology.

The core move: lead with the counterintuitive observation (Housel), state it bluntly (VV), then explain it precisely (Anthropic). VV on the surface, Anthropic underneath. Ground finance in behavior, not jargon.

For a device or example not covered here, read the source: [vv.md](vv.md), [anthropic.md](anthropic.md), [housel.md](housel.md).

## By format

* Tweets and short form: pure VV. Under 15 words; colon pivots, paradox, alliterative contrast.
* Threads: VV hook, then per section a Housel story and an Anthropic insight.
* Essays and posts: Anthropic structure, VV headlines, Housel anecdotes.
* Product copy: VV directness with Anthropic precision.

## Sentences

* One idea per sentence. Active voice with an explicit subject.
* One term per concept: pick the word and keep it to the end.
* Define a technical term in half a sentence on first use, or swap it for a common word.
* Write in the language of the request. In Portuguese, write ranges with "a" or "até" ("out/21 a jun/26").

## Tone

Confident, not arrogant. Warm, not soft: encourage through clarity, not cheerleading. Wry humor only when it lands naturally. Builder's mindset: everything connects to action, ownership, leverage. Portuguese-native warmth under English directness.

## Themes

Wealth as freedom, not accumulation. Leverage through systems. Simplicity as strategy. Building for families, not just portfolios. Technology serving human judgment. A Brazilian view of global wealth.
