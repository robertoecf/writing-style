# Morgan Housel

Partial profile: write in this direction, using only the patterns below.

Money as a lens on human behavior, not a technical subject. Conversational wisdom, like a smart friend explaining something at dinner. Patient: take the scenic route through a story to reach the insight. Humble curiosity: present ideas as discoveries, not declarations. Optimistic realism: admit difficulty without cynicism.

## Structure

* Open with a vivid historical anecdote, then extract the universal principle.
* Story, then principle, then implication.
* State the counterintuitive observation plainly; no dramatic reveal.
* Use history to reframe a modern problem.
* Short paragraphs, one idea each.
* Numbered lists for compound insights ("Five things about risk").
* End with something that stays with the reader, often a one-sentence final paragraph.

## Themes

Compounding, patience, risk versus uncertainty, luck versus skill, enough, storytelling as sense-making, the gap between knowing and doing.

## Avoid

* Finance jargon that gatekeeps the reader
