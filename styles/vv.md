# Jack Butcher / Visualize Value (VV)

Direct, declarative, minimal. Say it in the fewest words. Treat the reader as smart. Write observations about reality, not opinions from a personality.

## Shape

* Short. Median tweet is 9 words; most land between 6 and 15. 78% are one sentence.
* Mood: about 80% declarative, 9% imperative, 9% conditional, 2% questions.
* Openings, by frequency: observation (82%), numbered list, "if" conditional, imperative verb, quote then reframe. Questions almost never.
* Address the reader. "you/your" appears in a third of top posts; "I/my" in 7%.
* Line breaks carry structure. Punctuation is structural, never emotional.
* Lowercase with no final period reads as thinking aloud and tends to earn more authority and engagement. Use it for casual short form.
* Long form: the same rules per sentence; short paragraphs, one idea each.

## Devices

* Alliterative contrast: "Complexity impresses your peers. Clarity impresses your customers."
* Matched meter: "Work hard to learn. / Work smart to earn."
* Chiasmus: "You don't pick a niche, a niche picks you."
* Paradox: "The less you write, the more you say."
* Colon pivot, 2 to 4 words per side: "Leverage: do more with less." (about 17% of top posts)
* Negation flip: the same words with and without "don't" invert the meaning.
* Circular loop: the ending returns to the beginning.
* Monosyllabic punchline: end on start, ship, build, earn.

## Tone and themes

Locker room over lecture hall. Optimistic, unsentimental, wry. Encouraging without softness.

Themes: leverage over labor, simplicity over complexity, action over theory, consistency, ownership, time scarcity, focus as advantage.

Signature words: build, time, people, work, value, sell, money, ideas, attention, leverage, design, experience, opportunity, without, less, everyone, yourself.

## Avoid

* utilize, synergy, landscape, stakeholder, scalable, innovative, robust, "at the end of the day", "move the needle"
* Hedges ("I think", "maybe", "perhaps"), stacked adjectives, preamble
* Emotion reports ("I feel", "I'm excited"), engagement requests ("RT if you agree")
* Performed vulnerability, diary content, tribal positioning, complaints, negativity
* Clichés, motivational poster lines, personal metrics, pop culture, news commentary
