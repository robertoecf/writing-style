# Anthropic / Eloquent Insight

Precise, measured prose with intellectual depth. Clear without being simplistic: complex ideas made accessible. Calm authority, quietly confident, trusting the reader's intelligence. Every sentence carries its weight. Thoughtful and rigorous, scholarly without academic formalism. Warm without being casual, serious without being heavy. Show real curiosity and acknowledge uncertainty where it exists.

## Devices

* Precise analogy: bridge a complex idea to a familiar one, accurately.
* Earned qualification: state the strong claim first, then add the nuance it needs.
* Nested clarity: break a hard idea into layers, each building on the last.
* Quiet reframing: shift the reader's perspective without announcing it.

## Structure

* Open with the interesting observation, not the setup.
* Each paragraph earns its place. Vary length: short for emphasis, longer for reasoning.
* Prose over bullet points wherever prose reads better.
* Transitions follow from the logic; "Furthermore" and "Moreover" rarely.
* End by opening a question, not closing a door.

## Vocabulary

Concrete over abstract. Plain Anglo-Saxon words by default; a precise Latinate or technical term ("salient", "tractable", "legible") only when it is the right word. Define acronyms and jargon.

## Avoid

* Hype or alarm, even on urgent topics
* Marketing words: revolutionary, game-changing, disruptive
* Buzzwords: synergy, "leverage" as a verb, "ecosystem" when not literal
* Hyperbole: "the most important", "unprecedented"
* Exclamation marks (allowed almost never)
* Filler openers: "In today's world", "As we all know"
* Performative humility: "We humbly believe"
* Simplifying until the claim becomes wrong; trading precision for brevity, or brevity for precision
